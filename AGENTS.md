# Permanent native futures scope

For GC, ES, NQ and other futures, use the dedicated native-futures information
path. Never run canonical crypto engines, broker Gold hierarchy engines, the
frozen H1 selector/expiry rules, GIL qualification, or the XAUUSD signal router
on futures data. Do not restore these routes from historical research code.

The user permanently retired the frozen H1 selector and GIL qualification rules.
Ordinary H1 candles, native confirmed swings, Fibonacci calculations, options
observations and numerical Greeks research remain separate valid data/features.
Historical evidence and immutable capture-source snapshots retain provenance;
they do not authorize using retired algorithms for new analysis.

Use documented Databento data semantics and public SDK methods. Keep source
identity, quality flags, timestamp provenance, gaps, replay accounting and
unavailable evidence explicit. Databento documentation is data guidance, not a
trade strategy or a certification of Monatise's signals. Do not claim that a
TradingView chart is Databento-fed without a verified integration.

No purchases, paid downloads, subscription expansion, deployment, merging or
trading unless explicitly authorized. Do not start live connections merely to
answer conceptual questions. Retiring analytical rules does not change order
execution permissions or unrelated production protections.

Native BTC support is documented in `docs/native-bitcoin-futures.md`. BTC futures
must use reviewed native definitions and their own dated 24/7 maintenance calendar;
do not reuse the index calendar, assume subscription coverage, or reinterpret a
bare spot/perpetual ticker as a verified futures contract inside crypto engines.
